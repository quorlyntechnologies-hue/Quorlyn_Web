## Product Purpose

The public website introduces Quorlyn Technology Private Limited, explains its software engineering capabilities and working method, and lets a prospective client submit a project enquiry. A separate authenticated staff area manages those enquiries.

## Positioning

The existing source presents Quorlyn as a new, deliberately small engineering company in Kathmandu, Nepal, founded in 2026. Its emphasis is maintainable software, explicit trade-offs, direct engineer involvement, and ownership of delivered systems.

## Brand Commitments

The user explicitly requires the existing company content, themes, and official logo to remain authoritative. The approved redesign brief adds a dimensional version of the Q/T monogram and its dissolving squares, inspired by the supplied MotionSites specification and uploaded 3D logo image. The later theme preference makes the public ground soft off-white. The cleanup request removes generic typography, decorative gradients/glass/grids, repeated text cards and unsupported copy. The typography follow-up establishes IBM Plex Sans as one consistent interface family, with original wordmark lettering and actual code/reference values kept separate. Copy remains direct and factual; the single homepage 3D logo retains continuous, pausable motion on desktop and mobile.
